# Official Digimon Card Game sleeves

These images were downloaded from Bandai's official Digimon Card Game product
catalog and are used as cosmetic card backs in Aegis.

- `digimon-standard.webp` and `digimon-egg.webp`: standard blue and white
  Digimon Card Game card backs, respectively, sourced from
  [Game.kiwi's card gallery](https://game.kiwi/digimon-tcg/ex6-002-yokomon/).

- `official-2020-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Sleeves 2020](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/official_sleeve.php).
- `official-2021-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Sleeves 2021](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/official_sleeve_2021.php).
- `official-2021v2-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Sleeves 2021 Ver.2.0](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/official_sleeve_2021ver2.php).
- `official-2022-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Sleeves 2022](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/official_sleeve_2022.php).
- `official-2022v2-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Sleeves 2022 Ver.2.0](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/official_sleeve_2022ver2.php).
- `official-2023-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Sleeves 2023](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/sleeve_07.php).
- `official-2024v1-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Sleeves 2024 Ver.1.0](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/sleeve_08.php).
- `official-2024v2-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Sleeves 2024 Ver.2.0](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/sleeve_09.php).
- `official-2025v1-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Sleeves 2025 Ver.1.0](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/sleeve_10.php).
- `official-01-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Card Sleeves 01](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/sleeve_11.php).
- `official-02-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Card Sleeves 02](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/sleeve_12.php).
- `official-03-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Card Sleeves 03](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/sleeve_13.php).
  The four designs are two Digimon Story Time Stranger designs, Digimon Data
  Squad, and Digimon Beatbreak. Their sleeve ids
  (`official-03-adventure`, `official-03-chronicle`,
  `official-03-generations`, and `official-03-ghost-game`) predate the
  correct names. They stay unchanged because players' saved settings and decks
  store them.
- `official-04-*.webp`: cropped from the four-design product sheet for
  [Official Card Sleeves 04](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/sleeve-official04.php).
- `alphamon.webp`: [Official Card Sleeves Ver. Alphamon](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/official_sleeve_alphamon.php).
- `omnimon.webp`: [Official Card Sleeves Ver. Omnimon](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/official_sleeve_omnimon.php).
- `imperialdramon-paladin.webp`: [Official Card Sleeves Ver. Imperialdramon: Paladin Mode](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/official_sleeve_imperialdramon-paladin.php).
- `gold.webp`: [Official Card Sleeves Gold](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/official_sleeve_gold.php).
- `silver.webp`: [Official Card Sleeves Silver](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/official_sleeve_silver.php).
- `animation-series-25th.webp`: [Official Sleeves Digimon Animation Series 25th](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/sleeve_animation_series_25th.php).

Sheet designs whose print is landscape (`official-2024v2-adventure-partners`
and three `official-04-*` designs) are rotated a quarter turn
counterclockwise to fit the portrait card back.

Copyright belongs to Bandai and the credited Digimon rights holders. The files
must not be treated as original Aegis artwork.
